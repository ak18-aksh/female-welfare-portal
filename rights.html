Know Your Legal Rights

Laws that protect women at home, at work and in society.

Law Protects What it means
Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005 Women facing abuse at home Protection orders, right to residence and monetary relief
Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act, 2013 Working women Workplaces with 10 or more employees must have an Internal Committee to hear complaints
Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961 Brides and their families Giving or taking dowry is a punishable offence
Maternity Benefit Act, 1961 (amended 2017) Working mothers Paid maternity leave of up to 26 weeks for eligible women
Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987 Eligible persons, including women Free legal aid through legal services authorities

This page gives general information, not legal advice. For help with a specific case, contact your District Legal Services Authority or call 181.
