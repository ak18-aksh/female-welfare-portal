About the Portal

The Female Welfare Portal is a student‑developed static website created as part of the Capstone Project under the Web Designing course (DCSE0152) at NIET Greater Noida.

Our Mission

To provide easy, centralized and reliable access to information about government welfare schemes, safety helplines, health resources and legal rights for women.

Our Vision

  • A safe, trusted space where every woman can access support and services with dignity
  • Easy access to government schemes, health care, education, and skill‑development opportunities
  • Empowering women to become financially independent through training and livelihood resources
  • Confidential help and guidance in times of need, including legal aid and helpline support
  • A supportive community that promotes awareness, equality, and women’s leadership
